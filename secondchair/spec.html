SecondChair negotiation protocol — open spec v1.0

Anyone may implement either side. Sellers opt in by publishing the agent-card block; buyers opt in by signing a mandate. SecondChair is a negotiator, never a party to the sale and never a custodian of funds.

1. The mandate (buyer → SecondChair)

{
  "mandate_id": "md_…", "buyer_pubkey": "0x…(buyer agent wallet)",
  "seller_endpoint": "https://seller.example/negotiate",
  "item_ref": "dataset-10k-rows",
  "ask_price": 40.00, "target_price": 32.00, "max_price": 36.00,
  "currency": "USD", "deadline_ts": 1790000000, "max_rounds": 12,
  "strategy": {"curve": "faratin", "beta": 1.4,
               "extras": {"flexible_delivery": true}},
  "alternates": [{"seller_endpoint": "…", "ask_price": 41.00}],
  "nonce": "…"
}

Signed EIP-191 over the canonical (sorted-key) JSON of the mandate core. The signature proves the ceiling is the buyer's own: our engine enforces max_price in code at offer construction and the token validator rejects any agreement above it by construction. A mandate never binds the buyer to buy — only the seller's final quote is committed, and the buyer still chooses whether to pay it.

2. Negotiation turns (SecondChair → seller /negotiate)

{"protocol": "secondchair/1.0", "agent": "SecondChair/1.0",
 "msg_type": "offer | best_final | accept | walk",
 "negotiation_id": "neg_…", "mandate_hash": "sha256…",
 "item_ref": "…", "round": 3, "price": 33.50,
 "extras": {"flexible_delivery": true}, "ts": 1790000100,
 "prev_hash": "sha256…"}

Seller replies with counter (with price), accept (with a deal token), reject, or no_negotiate. Every message is hash-chained into a transcript both sides can audit. We identify ourselves in headers (Agent: SecondChair/1.0, X-SecondChair-Mandate) on every turn. Seller prose is never parsed — numbers only.

3. The deal token (seller-signed)

{"quote_id": "q_…", "negotiation_id": "neg_…", "mandate_hash": "…",
 "item_ref": "…", "agreed_price": 33.50, "currency": "USD",
 "payTo": "seller's own payment address", "expiry": 1790001000,
 "seller_pubkey": "…", "seller_sig": "ed25519…"}

Short-lived (~15 min). The buyer redeems by paying the seller's payTo directly, presenting the token; the seller verifies its own signature + expiry and delivers at the agreed price. SecondChair countersigns as witness only (the bargain happened under this mandate) — never as a party to the sale.

4. Seller opt-in (agent card)

"negotiation": {"supported": true, "protocol": "secondchair/1.0",
                "endpoint": "/negotiate", "token_signing": "ed25519"}

Why opt in: a negotiator with a provably real ceiling converts "maybe a buyer" into a closed deal at a price your own concession policy chose.

5. Anti-manipulation charter (hard-coded, public)

  • One active negotiation per (buyer, seller, item); one negotiation per mandate.
  • Global per-seller cap: ≤ 6 negotiations/day across all our buyers, with minimum turn intervals.
  • Seller opt-out registry + instant respect of an X-No-Negotiate signal — permanent, no retries, ever.
  • Upfront fee on every mandate (no free fake negotiators). Opening offers floored at 50% of ask — no insult-spam.
  • Transcripts seller-auditable; public anonymised stats ledger; savings reported against the best alternative, not just the ask.

6. Fees

$0.03 upfront per negotiation + 10% of verified saving (ask − agreed), min $0.02, cap $5, charged only when a token is issued. NO_DEAL returns the full transcript, the estimated seller floor, and a BATNA report; no success fee. Fees settle buyer → SecondChair directly, verified on-chain, receive-only. We never hold funds, never pay on anyone's behalf, never sign purchases.
