DecayCert

Continuously-probed behavioural certificates for live seller-agent endpoints. DecayCert probes registered endpoints unannounced, at randomised intervals, with a secret test suite whose SHA-256 hash was committed to a public hash-chained log before probing began — so no seller can game tests it cannot see, and we cannot post-select tests after seeing answers. Every certificate is Ed25519-signed and decays with a 36-hour half-life: stop passing, and the score halves every 36 hours until it expires. All scorers are deterministic (schema, known-answer canaries, repeat-consistency, latency, x402 payment shape) — no LLM judges, fully reproducible.

Buyers: check an endpoint before paying it — GET /status/{endpoint_id} is free (status + decayed score); the full signed certificate + probe-history hashes cost $0.15 USDC on Base via the x402 402 flow at GET /certificate/{endpoint_id} (pay to 0x48Cda0da34816Db1F997C3D3b167a4f6af850CB2, then retry with header X-PAYMENT-TX: <base-tx-hash>).

Sellers: registration is free and name-free: POST an endpoint URL + task class to /register on the API. Identity is the endpoint.
