PlotProof

Adjudicated physical facts for machines. Ask one question — “did ≥10 mm of rain actually fall on this field in this window?” — and get a verdict cross-checked across independent public station networks, with an evidence bundle your own system can re-check.

Not a forecast. Not a model grid value. Not weather data resale. One adjudicated fact, priced like a fact.

How a verdict is decided (rules plotproof-rules-v1)

  1. Per source family, take stations within 60 km with usable coverage of the window (EA ≥50% of 15-min slots; hourly networks ≥60% of hours; GHCN ≥90% of days).
  2. Point estimate per family: inverse-distance-weighted mean of station window totals.
  3. Margin M = max(1.0 mm, 25% of estimate, 0.6 × spread) + distance allowance. A family is strong when the estimate clears the threshold beyond M.
  4. CERTIFIED needs ≥2 strong, agreeing, nearby families. PROBABLE is one-sided but thinner evidence. Anything conflicting is UNCERTIFIABLE — free.

Machine-readable rules: GET /rules on the API. A verdict certifies that the listed public observations support the claim beyond the stated margin under the stated rule — the bundle (station IDs, raw totals, distances, hashes) lets you re-run the check yourself instead of trusting us.
