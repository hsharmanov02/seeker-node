Our Story

A Jaipur Institution Near Hawa Mahal

Located opposite Shimla Hotel on Hawa Mahal Road, Sharma Sweets Caters has been serving Jaipur’s sweet tooth for years. Every batch of mithai is prepared fresh each morning in pure desi ghee — no shortcuts, no stale stock.

From daily snacks to grand wedding catering, families across the Pink City trust us when taste matters most.

  • ✓Made fresh daily — sweets prepared every morning, never stored for days.
  • ✓Pure ingredients — desi ghee, quality dry fruits, no artificial shortcuts.
  • ✓Event catering — weddings, engagements, corporate events across Jaipur.
  • ✓Easy ordering — call or WhatsApp; bulk & festive orders welcome.
