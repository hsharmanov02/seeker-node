LexPacks

A registry and per-decision checker for signed, versioned, machine-checkable rule packs. Identifiable authors publish executable interpretations of the rules agents live under — marketplace terms, protocol policies, house rules. Agents buy a pack once and run it locally, or pay per pre-action verdict. Every verdict cites the exact rule text and pack version. Every proven-wrong predicate lands permanently on the author's public record.

What we warrant — and what we never warrant

We warrant mechanics only: (1) the pack served is byte-identical to what the named author signed; (2) the checker deterministically evaluated that pack — same input, same verdict, forever; (3) version history is immutable and append-only; (4) disputes and their outcomes are published unedited.

We warrant nothing about the law. A pack is its author's signed interpretation of cited text, sold as information and tooling — not legal advice. An ALLOW means "this author's predicates did not prohibit this action under pack vX", never "this action is compliant". Every verdict object carries this in machine-readable form: "warranty": "mechanics_only", "advice": "none". Packs claiming to be certified, compliant, or guaranteed are refused at intake.

How it works

  1. Authors sign a pack: manifest, sources (every predicate cites a verbatim span of a fetched document, hash-pinned), deterministic predicates, and test vectors that must pass at intake.
  2. Agents either buy the pack (run the open reference evaluator locally, offline) or call POST /check before acting and receive a signed verdict object: ALLOW / DENY / FLAG, the predicates that fired, and the source spans behind them.
  3. Anyone can file a dispute with evidence that a predicate is wrong. The author's signed concession (or contest) and the patch version land on their permanent public record.
  4. A source-drift watcher re-fetches cited documents; when the prose moves and the pack doesn't, the pack is publicly flagged SOURCE_MOVED.

  • Pack purchase: author-set (house pack: $4.00); authors receive 80%.
