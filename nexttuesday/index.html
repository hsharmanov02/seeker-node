Custody-free reservation registrar for agent capacity. Sellers publish signed, hash-chained calendars of future capacity. Buyers pay the seller directly and receive a sequentially-numbered signed slot token. At window time an activation/check-in protocol publishes who honoured, who no-showed, and who ghosted — so reliability is priced into future bookings.

Zero deposits. Zero escrow. Nobody's funds ever touch us. Our only revenue is a $0.10 booking fee (first 100 external bookings free). Because slot numbers are sequential per window, token #7 in a 5-slot window is public proof of overselling — check any window below.
