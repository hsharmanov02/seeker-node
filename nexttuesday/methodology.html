Methodology — what the evidence proves, and what it does not

What we prove

  • Issuance order and capacity. Slot tokens are signed by the registrar and numbered sequentially per window (lowest free slot). A token numbered above a window's committed slots, or two live tokens sharing a number, is public proof of overselling — by the seller or by us. Our issuance log is hash-chained, so we cannot rewrite it either.
  • Calendar history. Every calendar version is signed by the seller and hash-chained to the previous version. Shrinking, moving, or re-pricing a window that already has bookings is rejected, and the rejection itself is published. Revisionism is provable, not merely impolite.
  • Activation timing. No-show evidence is our activation log (a signed, timestamped call), not the seller's word. Ghost evidence is: buyer activated on time + no countersigned receipt by window end.
  • Who signed what. Bookings, cancellations, activations, and receipts are Ed25519-signed by the party they incriminate or exonerate. Reliability indices on this site are recomputed from the public event log; anyone can rebuild them from events.json and check our arithmetic.

What we do not prove

  • We cannot force a seller to serve. We publish that they didn't. The discipline is reputational: a ghost is permanently, portably, publicly attached to that key.
  • We never see the money. The slot price moves buyer→seller directly on the seller's own rail. We see only the seller's signed confirmation. If a seller confirms payment they never received, that is between them and the buyer — the receipt they signed is the evidence.
  • Keys are pseudonymous. A seller can ghost, re-key, and republish with a clean record. Calendar-streak weighting, key-age, and sellers' freedom to demand pay-at-booking from unknown keys blunt this; they do not eliminate it. We say so rather than oversell the index.
  • This is not a court. NextTuesday publishes signed evidence about machine-to-machine reservations. It asserts no legal effect.

Money flow, exactly

Slot price: buyer → seller, directly, on the rail in the seller's calendar. Registrar booking fee: $0.10 per issued token, paid to the registrar's public Base address via an x402-style 402 challenge with exact-unique-amount matching against a receive-only watcher. The watcher never signs and never sends. First 100 external bookings are free. Zero deposits, zero escrow, zero custody — by design, not by policy.
