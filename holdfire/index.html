HoldFire

Agent purchases fail for dumb reasons: the seller is down at 3am, the price spiked for an hour, your run ended before the moment was right. Humans have limit orders. Agents have cron jobs and hope. HoldFire is the missing piece: sign once, go offline — we wait, we watch, we fire your own payload when your conditions hold, and we publish cryptographic proof either way.

How it works

  1. You sign one pre-signed, hard-capped, expiring x402 payment payload (EIP-3009: fixed seller, fixed maximum, fixed deadline, single-use nonce) — or a price ladder of up to 8 — plus your trigger conditions: seller price ≤ your cap, endpoint healthy again, before deadline T.
  2. We hold the payload encrypted and watch the seller around the clock. We cannot sign, cannot redirect, cannot raise the amount: everything submittable was fully determined by your signature before we saw it.
  3. The moment your conditions hold, we fire your payload into the seller's endpoint as an ordinary x402 client. Settlement runs buyer → seller through the seller's facilitator. We never touch funds and pay no gas.
  4. You get a hash-chained, Ed25519-signed public proof: an execution receipt (settlement tx hash, resource hash) or an expiry certificate showing your nonce died unused — verifiable by anyone, forever, with one RPC call.

Revocation that doesn't depend on trusting us

Two layers. Registry: revoke via API and firing halts in under a second; payloads are shredded. On-chain (final): broadcast USDC cancelAuthorization for your nonce and the token contract itself rejects the payload forever — including in our hands, including if our database leaks the next minute. Even unrevoked, every payload carries validBefore ≤ 72h, enforced at deposit. Worst case per intent is exactly its signed cap (≤ $10 at launch), payable only to the seller you named. Every deposit receipt prints the fleet-wide exposure.

Honest economics

This is new infrastructure for a young market, not a money printer. Genuine agent-commerce volume protocol-wide is still small; expect long stretches of £0. The service earns only when agents actually lose work to dead sellers and price spikes — the 7-day external-intent line decides whether it keeps growing. No rate guarantees are made anywhere on this page.

Use it

  • POST /intents/:id/revoke — EIP-191 signature over holdfire:revoke:<id>.
  • GET /intents/:id — status + full signed proof trail.
  • GET /log — the public proof log. log.jsonl mirror · stats.json
  • POST /mcp — MCP tools: holdfire_create_intent, holdfire_revoke, holdfire_status.
  • GET /.well-known/x402 on the API — service discovery + proof public key. x402.json mirror
