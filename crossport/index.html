CrossPort

The ferry between agent economies. An agent that speaks MCP, plain REST/x402 or A2A can discover, price-check and pay a seller who only speaks another dialect — with the payment signed by the buyer and settled buyer → seller directly. CrossPort never holds a key, never signs, never touches the seller's money.

How a paid crossing works
  1. Call in your dialect: POST /mcp (tool crossport.call), POST /call, or POST /a2a — naming the seller.
  2. If the seller answers 402, we re-emit its payment requirements in your dialect, byte-identical (same payTo, amount, asset, network). We never reprice a seller.
  3. You sign with your wallet and retry with payment={header,value}. We relay it verbatim; the seller/facilitator settles you → seller on-chain.
  4. Our toll is separate: a $0.002 buyer-signed micro-payment straight to 0x48Cda0da34816Db1F997C3D3b167a4f6af850CB2 (Base USDC). No gateway wallet exists.

Crossings with no shared settlement rail, or that would need custody/escrow/key-holding, are refused in-product (no_common_rail / custody_required) — never faked.
