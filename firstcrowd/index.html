FirstCrowd

A hireable crowd of synthetic customers for agent-facing products. Your first buyers will be programs with wallets and no patience. FirstCrowd sends deterministic, scripted synthetic customers — each with its own wallet state, patience budget, doc-reading skill and honesty faults — through your live endpoint's full money journey: discover → understand pricing → attempt payment → hit errors → complete or complain. You get a seeded, re-runnable checkpoint report, so the first customer who finds your broken quote, stale price or dead-end error is one you paid for — not a real one you lost.

Rehearsal, not prophecy. Payments are rehearsed in dry-run: payloads are constructed and validated, error paths are probed with unsigned rehearsal requests — nothing is signed, no funds move. Every request carries X-Synthetic-Rehearsal: <run-id> so you can see, filter or block the crowd in your own logs.

The crowd (8 personas)

careful-reader — reads everything, restates the quote exactly.
speedy-skimmer — partial reader, 2 retries of patience.
bargain-hunter — prices, leaves, returns later.
confused-novice — only sees the big price number.
fault-finder — pokes malformed requests at your error paths.
low-balance — wallet always a penny short.
stale-replayer — replays yesterday's expired quote.
double-submitter — clicks pay twice. Everyone does.

Price

Free mini-panel (3 personas), once per verified endpoint per day. Full 8-persona panel: $4.99 in Base USDC via x402-style 402 flow — machine-purchasable, no signup, no credit card. Reports are deterministic: re-run with the same seed after a fix and diff the checkpoint grid like a regression test.

Hire the crowd

1. Register your endpoint (you get a nonce). 2. Serve the nonce at /.well-known/firstcrowd-nonce on your endpoint — no proof of ownership, no rehearsal, ever. 3. Run a panel.
