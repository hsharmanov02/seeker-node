How it works

  1. Pay the exact USDC amount shown to the Base address (USDC on Base network).
  2. Open the pre-filled order issue and paste your payment transaction hash — a watcher verifies the transfer against the public chain record automatically.
  3. Your report (PDF + JSON + verification page) is generated and linked on the issue, typically within ~15 minutes.
