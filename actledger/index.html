How it works

  1. You (or your agent) sign a declaration naming the agent's identities and sources. No account, no email, no private keys — ever.
  2. ActLedger reads the declared public chains and signed registries and normalizes everything into anchored "acts": payments, commitments, deals, certificates, outcomes.
  3. At period close you receive a signed statement whose every line can be independently re-verified against its anchor. Late evidence arrives as an addendum; a closed statement is never silently rewritten.
  4. What might be missing — orphan payments, orphan commitments, possible undeclared identities, silent sources — is listed in a known-unknowns annex as hints, never folded into the totals.
